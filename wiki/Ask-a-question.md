# 🙋 Ask a question

**Didn't find the answer? Here is how to ask.**

---

Follow these steps, in this order.

1. Read the [frequently asked questions](Frequently-asked-questions). The answer may already be there.
2. Look through the [question issues already open](https://github.com/fezarosa-dev/joBot/issues?q=label%3Aquestion). Someone may have asked the same thing.
3. Did not find it? Open an issue with the [question form](https://github.com/fezarosa-dev/joBot/issues/new?template=duvida.yml).

## ⚠️ Things to keep in mind

- A **security flaw** does not go in a public issue. Use the [private report](https://github.com/fezarosa-dev/joBot/security/advisories/new).
- Do not put personal data (name, email, password, résumé) in the issue.
- You can write in Portuguese or English.
